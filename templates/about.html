WHAT IS SIGNMARK?

Making Communication Accessible

SignMark is an AI-based American Sign Language learning project that helps users explore, learn, and practice ASL alphabet signs.

Through sign recognition, an interactive alphabet dictionary, and educational quizzes, the platform creates a simple learning experience for beginners and anyone interested in sign language.

🤟

Communication For Everyone

Learn signs. Understand gestures. Connect with people.
